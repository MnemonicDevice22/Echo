How to use

Capture: type an artist, and an album and song if you have them. Tap Pending for recommendations you haven't heard yet.

Crate: artists, then their albums, then songs. Songs without an album sit under the artist. Tap a song for its note. The circle on a song marks it pending; a violet outline shows pending songs inside. The bar on each row goes from bright to dim the longer since you opened it.

Dig: three rows for artists, albums and songs, each starting with what you've opened least recently. Swipe a row or use its arrows. Tap an artist or album to mark it revisited; tap a song to open it.
