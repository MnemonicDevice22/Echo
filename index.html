How to use

Capture: type an artist and, if you have them, the song or album and where you heard it. Anything works; tidy it later.

Crate: your archive, alphabetical. The bar on each card shows how recently you opened it, bright to dim. Search, or tap a tag to filter.

Dig: walks through the archive starting with what you've opened least recently. Swipe or use the arrows. Opening a card moves it to the back of the line.
